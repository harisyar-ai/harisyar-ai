Publications

Domain-Specific Feature Engineering and Explainable Machine Learning for Used Vehicle Valuation in Pakistan

Submitted to International Journal of Data Science & Analytics · 2026

Benchmarked 13 ML models on 58,750 PakWheels listings for used car price prediction in Pakistan. LightGBM achieved R² = 0.9676. Introduced domain-specific features (vehicle generation encoding, brand origin, city tier, trim grade) validated via SHAP analysis. Deployed as a public Streamlit app. Manuscript under review at International Journal of Data Science & Analytics.
